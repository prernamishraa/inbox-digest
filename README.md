# Inbox Digest

Stop missing the best of Substack.

Inbox Digest fetches your Substack subscriptions, ranks the top posts worth reading this week, and delivers a personalised digest to your inbox every morning. Built for people who over-subscribe and never have enough time to read everything.

## What it does

- Paste your Substack profile URL
- App fetches all your subscriptions automatically
- Claude reads and ranks the top posts worth your time
- Digest delivered to your inbox every morning
- Discover new Substacks based on what you already read

## Screens

- **Screen 1** — Landing page with Substack URL input and category selection
- **Screen 2** — Personalised onboarding with animated loading messages
- **Screen 3** — Digest page with ranked posts, summaries and recommendations

## Tech stack

- Next.js 16
- Convex (database and backend)
- Vercel (deployment)
- Claude API (digest generation and ranking)
- Resend (email delivery)
